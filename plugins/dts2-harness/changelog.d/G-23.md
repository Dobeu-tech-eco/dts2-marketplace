### Changed
- G-23: ledger and metrics rows share one event shape `{v, ts, run, lane, kind, id}` (`state_io.make_event`, `State.append_event`). An event written with a caller-chosen id (`event_id(...)`) is recorded once: council ballots and a metrics stamp no longer duplicate on a re-run. `append_text` stays the O_APPEND primitive.
