### Changed
- G-11: the lane lock moves into the state port (`Store.lock`): stale locks are reclaimed by rename under a reclaim guard, a holder can `renew()`, and a reclaimed holder never frees its successor's lock. `State.acquire_lease` and `release_lease` require `epoch=` (`EpochRequired`, `StaleEpoch`), and `State.sendback` counts with an exclusive create so racing callers never share a number.
