### Added
- G-13: `lanes.py transition <stamp> --lane L --to PHASE` is the sole writer of a lane's phase. One `LEGAL` table (starting, pr-open, done, blocked, incomplete, orphaned) rejects illegal moves with the list of legal ones; `heartbeat --status`, park, stall and spawn all go through it. A son may report only pr-open, done, blocked or incomplete. `lanes.py shutdown <stamp>` is the graceful end of a run: it parks running lanes (reason `shutdown`), stops their sons (SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after a grace period), closes leases, returns budget shares and restores git gc.
