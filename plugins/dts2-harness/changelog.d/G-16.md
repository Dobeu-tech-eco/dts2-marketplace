### Added
- G-16: leases carry `expires_at` and `ttl_s` (twice `max_wall_minutes`); a heartbeat renews them. Expired or closed leases of other runs stop blocking new leases. The overlap check and the lease write are one step under a single admission lock. A lease also records the `session_id` (from `CLAUDE_SESSION_ID`) when known, for the SessionEnd hook.
